# Amigus

## Summary

The pandemic forced students to study from home, increasing isolation. **Amigus** is a social robot that provides companionship, motivational messages and tasks to students during online classes, supporting mental health and emotional well-being.

## What it does

- **Mental health support:** offers companionship to reduce isolation.
- **Emotion identification:** users can input their emotional state through buttons or gesture recognition and receive empathetic eye expressions.
- **Encouraging recordings:** the robot responds with audio phrases and movie quotes adapted to the detected emotion.
- **Stimulates communication:** asks questions during the day to promote self-knowledge and social interaction.
- **Trustworthy companion:** encourages users to call or write friends, and includes touch interactions such as rubbing its back or head.

## Publication

This project was a finalist at the **ICSR 2021 Robot Design Competition** in Singapore (remote) and later published at **HRI 2022 (ACM/IEEE International Conference on Human-Robot Interaction)**, also held remotely in Singapore.
